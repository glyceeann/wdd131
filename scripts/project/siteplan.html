Website Planning Document

Riyadh Weekend Planner

A Family Guide to Weekends in Riyadh

Site Name

Riyadh Weekend Planner — the name tells visitors right away what the site does: it helps you plan a weekend in Riyadh. The logo will appear in the header of every page.

Optional domain availability: riyadhweekendplanner.org

Site Purpose

The site is a small informational website to help visitors plan fun, easy, family-friendly weekends in Riyadh. It provides a searchable list of real places in Riyadh — parks, museums, malls with play areas, and outdoor spots — plus an interactive weekend picker and a “Plan My Weekend” form.

Visitors can filter places by type (Indoor / Outdoor), cost (Free / Paid), and area. They can choose their budget, type of activity, and number of people to get a suggested plan, and save their favorite places. A References page linked in the footer will credit any image sources.

Pages: Home, Places to Go, Plan My Weekend, References

Scenarios

  • “We have two kids and one free Saturday — what free outdoor place can we visit in Riyadh?”
    They will use Places to Go and filter by Outdoor + Free + Area to find a park that fits their family.
  • “We are visiting Riyadh for the first time and have a small budget — what should we do this weekend?”
    They will use the “What should we do this weekend?” picker on Home or the Plan My Weekend form to get a suggestion based on budget and activity type.
  • “It is too hot to stay outside — where can we go indoors with kids?”
    They will filter Places to Go by Indoor to find museums and malls with play areas, then save their favorites for later.

Color Schema

The colors are inspired by Riyadh — deep green and warm desert sand — and will be used consistently across the whole site: headings, buttons, header/footer, cards, and backgrounds.

#0F4C4C Headings, Header, Footer, Buttons
#C9A86A Accents, Borders, Highlights
#FFF8EE Page Background
#222222 Paragraph Text

Typography

  • Headings — Playfair Display, serif: Used for the site name, page titles, and section headings. It feels welcoming and a little elegant.
  • Body — Roboto, sans-serif: Used for paragraphs, lists, cards, navigation, and form labels. It is easy to read on mobile and desktop.
